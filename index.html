What is NVIDIA AI?

A simple introduction for students

NVIDIA AI refers to technologies and platforms that help developers and organisations build and run artificial intelligence applications.

These technologies support areas such as machine learning, generative AI, computer vision, robotics, simulations and high-performance computing.

This educational website explains important concepts in a simple and interactive way.

Did you know?

AI systems can learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make predictions, generate content or assist people with complex tasks.

Important AI Technologies

Explore the main areas connected with NVIDIA AI

Deep Learning

Deep learning uses neural networks to identify patterns in images, text, sound and other types of data.

⚡

Accelerated Computing

Specialised processors can speed up demanding calculations used in scientific computing and artificial intelligence.

🤖

Generative AI

Generative AI can produce text, images, code, audio and other content based on user instructions.

👁️

Computer Vision

Computer vision allows computers to understand information from photographs, videos and cameras.

🦾

Robotics

AI can help robots understand their environment, plan actions and perform useful tasks.

🌍

Digital Twins

Digital twins are computer-based models that can represent real-world objects, systems or environments.
